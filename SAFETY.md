# Child Safety Standards & CSAM Prevention Policy

**Minibee Viewer** is an open-source client application for connecting to the Second Life virtual world network operated by Linden Lab. We are committed to supporting a safe online environment and strictly prohibit any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

## 1. Zero Tolerance Policy
Minibee Viewer has a zero-tolerance policy regarding any illegal content involving minors. Users are strictly prohibited from using this application to transmit, view, store, or share CSAM/CSAE or any material that exploits or endangers children.

## 2. Platform Rules & Enforcement
Because Minibee Viewer interfaces with Linden Research, Inc. (Linden Lab) infrastructure:
* All users must strictly comply with the [Second Life Terms of Service](https://www.lindenlab.com/tos) and Community Standards regarding child safety.
* Accounts found violating child safety rules on the Second Life grid are subject to immediate termination by Linden Lab.

## 3. Reporting & Safety Mechanisms
* **In-App Reporting:** Users can report avatars, chat abuse, or inappropriate content directly to Linden Lab grid moderators through the standard Second Life Abuse Report system.
* **Direct Contact:** Safety concerns or reports regarding the Minibee Viewer application itself can be sent directly to the project maintainer at `panterapolnocy@gmail.com`.

## 4. Compliance & Cooperation
We cooperate fully with relevant law enforcement agencies and organization guidelines to report and prevent child sexual exploitation and abuse online.
